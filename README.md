# Superstore Sales Analysis | SQL (EDA)

## Overview
This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) using SQL on the Superstore dataset.  
The goal is to extract business insights related to sales, customers, shipping, and profitability.

---

## Tools Used
- SQL Server
- SQL Queries (T-SQL)
- Data stored in CSV format
- Data Analysis (EDA techniques)

---

## Dataset
The dataset contains sales transactions including:
- Orders
- Customers
- Sales
- Profit
- Shipping details
- Regions and Cities

---

## Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Sales by City
- Identified top-performing cities based on total sales
- Calculated percentage contribution of each city to overall sales

---

### 2. Customer Transactions Analysis
- Measured number of transactions per customer
- Identified most active customers

---

### 3. Sales by Segment
- Compared sales performance across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments

---

### 4. Customer Segmentation
- Created customer levels using SQL CASE statement:
  - VIP Customers
  - Average Customers
  - Normal Customers

---

### 5. Shipping Analysis
- Calculated shipping duration using DATEDIFF
- Analyzed average shipping days per region

---

### 6. Sales Over Time
- Analyzed yearly sales trends
- Identified growth patterns across years

---

### 7. Top Customers by Sales
- Identified top 10 customers contributing highest revenue

---

### 8. Category Performance
- Compared Sales vs Profit across product categories:
  - Technology
  - Furniture
  - Office Supplies

---

### 9. High Sales but Low Profit Cities
- Identified cities with high revenue but low profitability

---

## Key Insights
- Sales increased significantly over time, showing positive growth trend
- Certain cities generate high sales but low profit due to operational costs
- Technology category generates highest revenue
- Shipping delays vary across regions
- A small group of customers contributes significantly to total sales

---

## Skills Demonstrated
- SQL Data Analysis (EDA)
- Aggregations (SUM, COUNT, AVG)
- Data Filtering and Grouping
- CASE statements for segmentation
- Window-level business insights
- Business problem solving using SQL

---

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to extract meaningful business insights from raw transactional data and support data-driven decision making.
